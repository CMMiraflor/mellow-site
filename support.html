Help with the Mellow app for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Common questions

I cannot hear Mellow or the lesson sounds.

Turn the volume up with the buttons on the side of your device. In a lesson, the speaker button in the top corner switches Mellow's voice on and off. Mellow's voice uses your device's built-in text-to-speech, so it also follows your device's speech settings.

How do I choose a different age group?

Open Syllabus and tap one of the groups along the top: Little Maestros, Young Musicians, Music Explorers, Rising Artists or Lifelong Musicians. Progress in each group is kept separately.

Why is a lesson locked?

Lessons open in order. Finish the lesson before it and the next one unlocks.

Does Mellow listen to my playing?

No. Mellow does not use the microphone. For practice on a real violin, you tell Mellow when you have done it by tapping "I Did It!".

How do I start again?

Open Progress and choose Reset progress. This erases all completed lessons and achievements on that device and cannot be undone.

Is my progress saved if I get a new device?

Progress is saved only on the device. There are no accounts, so it does not sync between devices. A device backup may carry it over when you restore to a new device.
