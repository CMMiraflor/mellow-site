Mellow: Your Music Companion

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Mellow: Your Music Companion ("Mellow") is a music and violin learning app made by Ascented. This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version

Mellow does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

What Mellow saves on your device

To remember where a learner is, the app saves three things on the device it is installed on:

  • The learner group chosen at the start. This is an age range such as "6 to 8", not a name, an age or a date of birth.
  • Which lessons have been finished, and the date each one was finished.
  • Whether Mellow's voice is switched on or off.

The app uses this to show progress, streaks and achievements. It is never sent to Ascented or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

What Mellow does not do

  • It does not ask for a name, an email address or any account.
  • It does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.
  • It does not show advertising.
  • It does not include analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not have in-app purchases.
  • It does not link out to websites or social media.
  • It does not need an internet connection, and it sends nothing over the internet.

Mellow's voice is produced by the text-to-speech feature built into your device.

Children

Mellow is designed for learners from age 3 upwards, including young children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. A parent or guardian does not need to create an account or provide any details for a child to use it.

Deleting saved progress

You can erase everything the app has saved at any time:

  • In the app, open Progress and choose Reset progress.
  • Or delete the app from the device.

Device backups

If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, the backup may include Mellow's saved progress along with your other app data. Those backups are controlled by your Apple account and settings. Ascented has no access to them.

Information from the App Store

Apple may share general statistics with app developers, such as how many times an app was downloaded, and crash reports from people who have chosen to share them with developers. This information does not identify you and is handled under Apple's own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Mellow changes what information the app handles, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
